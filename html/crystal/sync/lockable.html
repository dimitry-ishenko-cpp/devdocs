module Sync::Lockable

Overview

General type to abstract lockable types such as Sync::Mutex and Sync::RWLock to be used interchangeably by other types, for example Sync::ConditionVariable.

Direct including types

  • Sync::Exclusive(T)
  • Sync::Mutex
