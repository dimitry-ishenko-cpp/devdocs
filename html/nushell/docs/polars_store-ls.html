polars store-ls for dataframe

Lists stored polars objects.

Signature

> polars store-ls {flags}

Examples

Creates a new dataframe and shows it in the dataframe list

> let test = ([[a b];[1 2] [3 4]] | polars into-df);
    polars store-ls
