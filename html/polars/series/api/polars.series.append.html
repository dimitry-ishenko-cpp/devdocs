polars.Series.append

Series.append(
    other: Series,
) → Self

Append a Series to this one.

The resulting series will consist of multiple chunks.

Parameters:
other

Series to append.

Warning

This method modifies the series in-place. The series is returned for convenience only.

See also

extend

Examples

>>> a = pl.Series("a", [1, 2, 3])
>>> b = pl.Series("b", [4, 5])
>>> a.append(b)
shape: (5,)
Series: 'a' [i64]
[
    1
    2
    3
    4
    5
]

The resulting series will consist of multiple chunks.

>>> a.n_chunks()
2
