polars.Series.radians

Series.radians() → Series

Convert from degrees to radians.

Examples

>>> s = pl.Series("a", [-720, -540, -360, -180, 0, 180, 360, 540, 720])
>>> s.radians()
shape: (9,)
Series: 'a' [f64]
[
    -12.566371
    -9.424778
    -6.283185
    -3.141593
    0.0
    3.141593
    6.283185
    9.424778
    12.566371
]
