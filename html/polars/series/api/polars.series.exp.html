polars.Series.exp

Series.exp() → Series

Compute the exponential, element-wise.

Examples

>>> s = pl.Series([1, 2, 3])
>>> s.exp()
shape: (3,)
Series: '' [f64]
[
    2.718282
    7.389056
    20.085537
]
