torch.mtia.manual_seed_all

torch.mtia.manual_seed_all(seed) [source]

Sets the seed for generating random numbers on all MTIA devices. It’s safe to call this function if MTIA is not available; in that case, it is silently ignored.

Parameters:

seed (int) – The desired seed.
