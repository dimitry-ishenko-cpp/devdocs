torch.fx.experimental.unification.unification_tools.keymap

torch.fx.experimental.unification.unification_tools.keymap(func, d, factory=<class 'dict'>) [source]

>>> bills = {"Alice": [20, 15, 30], "Bob": [10, 35]}
