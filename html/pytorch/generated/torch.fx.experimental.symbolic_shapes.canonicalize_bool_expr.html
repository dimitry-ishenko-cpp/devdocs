torch.fx.experimental.symbolic_shapes.canonicalize_bool_expr

torch.fx.experimental.symbolic_shapes.canonicalize_bool_expr(expr) [source]

Canonicalize a boolean expression by transforming it into a lt / le inequality and moving all the non-constant terms to the rhs. We canonicalize And / Ors / Not via cnf and then canonicalize their subexpr recursively nb. sympy.Rel.canonical is not good enough sympy/sympy#25924

Parameters

expr (sympy.Expr) – Expression to canonicalize
