duration

Represents a positive or negative span of time.

Constructor

Creates a new duration.

You can specify the duration using weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds. You can also get a duration by subtracting two datetimes.

View example
#duration(
  days: 3,
  hours: 12,
).hours()
