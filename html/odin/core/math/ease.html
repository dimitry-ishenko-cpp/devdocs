package core:math/ease

Overview

Easing procedures used for animations.

Inverse easing procedures These are the mathematical inverses of the corresponding easing functions, allowing you to reverse the transformation: if y = ease_fn(x), then x = ease_fn_inverse(y) + some_imprecision
