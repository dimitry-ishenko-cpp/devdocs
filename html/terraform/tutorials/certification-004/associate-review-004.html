This guide lists the specific exam objectives and resources you can study to demonstrate those objectives. Review our learning path for a start-to-finish list of resources you can use to prepare for the exam. While the tutorials listed may use specific cloud providers to introduce the learning objectives, provider-specific knowledge is not necessary for the exam.
