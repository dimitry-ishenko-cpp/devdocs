Operators

Terraform policy supports the following operators for writing policy conditions. Terraform policy operators behave like their Terraform counterparts. Refer to Arithmetic and logical operators for more information.

Note

This functionality is in beta. Do not use beta features in production environments.

Arithmetic operators

  • : Addition
  • : Subtraction and negation
  • : Multiplication
  • : Division
  • : Modulo division

Comparison operators

  • : Equal to
  • : Not equal to
  • : Less than
  • : Greater than
  • : Less than or equal to
  • : Greater than or equal to

Logical Operators

  • : Logical and
  • : Logical or
  • : Logical not
